## Summary

Most edge cases can be handled with good abstractions. The key insight is that tailwind errors fall into two categories: retryable and terminal. Handle them differently.

## Key points

- tailwind and git are often used together, but they solve different problems
- The ecosystem is mature - prefer well-maintained libraries over rolling your own
- Community plugins vary wildly in quality - check maintenance status before depending on them
